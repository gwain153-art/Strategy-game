REAL-TIME STRATEGY · SINGLE PLAYER

Opposing force

Theatre size

Objective. Destroy the enemy headquarters.

Expand. Engineers found towns. Manpower is capped by how many towns you hold, so a single-town force hits a ceiling it can never break.

Research. Vehicles do not exist until you unlock them. Research needs Research Labs, and higher tiers need more labs. Armour needs a Motor Pool, then a Tank Factory. Guns need an Artillery Park.

Combine arms. Anti-tank teams gut vehicles and melt to infantry. Tanks flatten infantry and get outranged by guns. Artillery hits hard and cannot fire at anything in its face.

Controls. Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap the minimap to jump. Tap a unit to select it, tap again for every unit of that type on screen, hold a spot to attack-move.
